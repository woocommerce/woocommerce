# Activity Panels

Activity panels surface actionable store information in the **Your store today** section of WooCommerce > Home. They appear after the initial setup task list is hidden.

The available panels depend on the store's products, orders, inventory settings, and review settings.

## Orders Panel

The Orders panel lists orders with statuses that are considered actionable and links to the full orders list.

Manage actionable order statuses at WooCommerce > Analytics > Settings.

## Stock Panel

For stores with stock management enabled, the Stock panel lists products that are low in stock or out of stock. You can update product quantities directly from the panel.

Manage stock settings at WooCommerce > Settings > Products > Inventory.

## Reviews Panel

For stores with product reviews enabled, the Reviews panel lists reviews that are pending moderation and links to the full reviews list.

Manage product review settings at WooCommerce > Settings > Products.
