# Payouts and revenue share

This page explains how revenue share works on the Woo Marketplace, when you are paid, and how to get set up to receive payouts. The commercial terms are in the [Marketplace Vendor Agreement](https://woocommerce.com/woo-vendor-agreement/). If this page and the agreement differ, the agreement applies.

## How revenue share works

You receive 70% of the Product Net Revenue from sales of your products on the Woo Marketplace. Product Net Revenue is the amount customers pay for your product, minus:

- Affiliate costs
- Refunds and reversals
- Payment processing fees
- Taxes

Joining the Marketplace and listing a product is free. Woo's share is the commission described in the agreement.


## When you are paid

We send payouts on the 10th of each month for the previous calendar month's sales. If the 10th falls on the weekend, payouts are attempted on the previous business day.

Small balances are not lost. If your balance is below the payout minimum, it stays on your profile and is included in a later payout. The minimum varies by currency.

## How you are paid

We pay vendors through Tipalti. We will send you an invite to the supplier portal when your first submission clears UX review. 

A link to the portal is at **Products > Payouts History** in the vendor dashboard.

### Supported countries and currencies

See [Tipalti](https://help.tipalti.com/hc/en-us/articles/31314361313815-Payment-methods-coverage-US-ROW) for what countries and payout methods and currencies are available. 

### Business name or personal name

When you join the Marketplace, we ask for your **Business Legal Name** and **Business Contact Address** so we can set up your account on our accounting platform. These fields can be edited at **Settings > Store** for approved vendors. See [Managing your account](/docs/woo-marketplace/managing-your-account/#business-legal-name-and-contact-address).

If you will be paid to a bank account that uses your personal name rather than a registered business name, please use your name as your **Business Legal Name**.

## Refunds

Customer refunds reduce your Product Net Revenue for the period. We don't claw refunded amounts back from your bank account. Instead, a refund is applied as a negative credit against your payouts. 


## Taxes

We remove sales taxes collected from customers before calculating Product Net Revenue and remit accordingly.

You are responsible for your own taxes on income from sales on the Woo Marketplace. 

We will ask for tax forms dependent on your location. These forms required, and we will not be able to pay you if they are not complete or have expired.  

## Fees

There is no fee to join the Marketplace or list a product.

Woo's commission is the revenue share described in the agreement.

WooCommerce.com accepts payments in multiple currencies, and each are converted to USD at the time of the transaction. This may affect the Product Net Revenue, and therefore your commission. 

## Changes to your business details

If you change your business name during the onboarding process, you'll be asked to resign the Partner Agreement in your vendor dashboard. If the business changes to a new legal entity after you start selling, we set up a new accounting profile and payouts move to the new entity. Please contact us in this instance. See [Managing your account](/docs/woo-marketplace/managing-your-account/).

## Questions about payouts

For questions about a specific payout, contact the accounting contact listed in your vendor dashboard at **Products > Payouts History**.

For questions about your products or account, use the support link in your vendor dashboard.
